    Getting started
    
  
    1. Add artwork. Drop PNG, WebP or GIF artwork onto the sheet, paste an image, or click Add artwork.
    2. Arrange. Drag a sticker to move it, use the bottom-right handle to resize, or enter its dimensions in the sticker list.
    3. Check the cut. The coloured outline shows what the blade will follow. Use Border to add space outside the image.
    4. Connect and print. Connect the PixCut S1 over USB in a supported Chromium browser, then select Print & cut.
    
  
    Artwork and cut paths
    
  
    A fully opaque image normally cuts as a rectangle. Use a transparent background for shaped stickers. A fully opaque sheet cannot provide an internal cut outline. Tiny shapes or crowded sheets may need adjustment; BrenCut refuses jobs when it cannot generate a usable cut.
    
  
    Simple, Kawaii and Engineer
    
  
    The appearance buttons change colours, typography and spacing only. They do not change image scale, cutter pressure, sheet layout or printer commands. Your selection is remembered in this browser.
    
  
    Printer connection
    
  
    Use Chrome or Edge on a secure website or localhost. Close other software using the printer before connecting. Export the JPEG and PLT files if you only need the prepared artwork and cut path.
